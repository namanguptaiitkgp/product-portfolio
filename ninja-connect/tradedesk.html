SSituation · The mess

Process inefficiencies were holding back scale.

In Q2 2023, Ninja Global — Ninjacart's global business vertical — set out to build a single trade system that could handle every touchpoint of a trade, from generating a lead to closing the deal and settling payments. A strategic bet on the scale and efficiency it could unlock if built properly.

The reality on the ground: nearly 85% of the communication between Buyers, Sellers and Ninjacart happened over unorganised WhatsApp groups — demand & supply collection, matching, negotiation, tracking, every part of the trade — backed by Excel sheets. It worked, barely, and it could not scale.

~85% of Buyer–Seller–Ninjacart trade ran on unorganised WhatsApp groups & Excel
TTask · The mandate

Break the trade — and build it again.

The brief from the GPM, reinforced by the co-founders and business heads: build and launch a single trade system swiftly and at scale. As the sole designer, the responsibility — and the potential business impact — sat with the design. The philosophy: break the trade into its pieces and rebuild each one into an open, scalable system that could be plugged into any use-case.

The design principles
1
Familiarity over novelty
A WhatsApp-like design so the system felt instantly usable — adoption over cleverness.
2
One open, scalable system
Built to plug into any trade use-case across the business, not a single bespoke flow.
3
Every touchpoint, one surface
Lead → match → negotiate → trade → payment, all handled in one place.
4
Web-responsive for the team
A responsive web build for Ninjacart's internal teams alongside the mobile experience.
Familiarity over novelty · web-responsive for internal teams · mapped to the NorthStar principles
AAction · The build

Four modules, one trade network.

The trade was rebuilt as four connected modules — pre-trade, the chat-based trade, post-trade payments, and the ERP dashboard.

Module 01 · The Pre-Trade

Getting in the supply & demand

Every trade begins with a supply and a demand. Since WhatsApp was the primary, convenient channel for sharing them, the pre-trade module captured that input in a structured way: a supply details page to share and track supplies in a few clicks, with the ability to define a status against each individual supply.

Capturing supply & demand · the supply details page · share, track & status
Module 02 · The Trade

A central, chat-based trade

The heart of the platform: a central chat-based trade system with custom built-in functionalities. Buyers and sellers give consent to start the trade, see live trade information, and customers handle order acceptance — all maintained in the internal system. It supports both the Platform-Model and Bill-to / Ship-to trade structures.

Chat-based trade · consent · live status · order acceptance · Platform & Bill-to/Ship-to models
Module 03 · The Post-Trade

Make payments with ease

An end-to-end in-app payment module handles payments for trades (and any loans a user holds): the buyer registers the payment and the seller accepts or rejects it. For edge cases — imports, exports, and very high-value payments — users can pay through an external process and simply make an outside-payment entry in-app.

Two ways to pay — in-app (buyer registers, seller accepts) & outside-payment entries
Module 04 · The Dashboard

Powerful ERP, in one place

The system is built on powerful ERP capabilities: track and manage the entire lead inventory, a full section to onboard, track and maintain the customer base, track, manage and apply credits, and an efficient task module to manage your team's and your own tasks — down to a sample email thread inside Tasks.

Lead inventory · customer onboarding · credits · the task module
