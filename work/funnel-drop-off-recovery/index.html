Case study · Digio

The Retargeting Module — winning back the almost-customers.

Most KYC drop-offs never come back on their own. This finds who stalled, where, and sends the one nudge that restarts them.

Role Senior Product Manager — DigiStudio  ·  Period 2024–present

View the interactive case study ↗

Helping you convert the customers who dropped off mid-onboarding — by showing exactly where they left, reaching them with a reminder, and proving what came back.

Every drop-off is a customer you already won.

Onboarding is a multi-step journey — PAN, Aadhaar, selfie, bank checks. A user can fall out at any one of them, and most of the ones who fall out had already chosen you. Yet once they left, there was no way to see where they stopped, and no lever to bring them back.

Find them. Reach them. Bring them back.

Build a module that converts dropped-off customers during onboarding — giving the onboarding and risk teams a single surface to see the leak, act on it, and measure the return. Three jobs, one workflow.

It lives where your workflows already do.

DigiStudio — the KYC orchestration platform — already lists every workflow, ranked by request volume. The Retargeting Module sits one click away: open a workflow's analytics and the whole re-engagement surface is right there.

See exactly where they fall off.

Workflow Analytics is the module's landing page — a Sankey-style view of conversion at each step of the journey. Click any action block and it breaks down the numbers for that single step, so the leak is no longer a mystery.

Turn a drop-off into a conversation.

Open any step and two actions are right there. Download the full list of users who dropped at that exact step — or send them a reminder directly, without ever leaving the chart.

Reach everyone who's still pending.

The buttons across the top send reminders and open performance reports. Send reminders to all opens a side panel to message every user with incomplete KYC inside a time range you choose — a whole cohort, in one move.

Every nudge, on the record.

A reminder history logs every campaign the team has run. Open any reminder group and you can check the status of each user who was sent that reminder — a clean, auditable trail of who was contacted and what happened next.

Proof that the nudge worked.

The Impact Dashboard shows what happened after the reminders went out — the conversion rate following each subsequent reminder, and a user's status flipping to complete the moment they finish their KYC.

Learn which channel earns it back.

A dedicated section in the dashboard breaks effectiveness down by communication channel — so every campaign is smarter than the last, and the budget follows the channel that actually converts.

See the drop. Send the nudge. Watch them convert.
